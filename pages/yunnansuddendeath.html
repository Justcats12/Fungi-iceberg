Yunnan sudden death syndrome

← Back

Yunnan is considered the place to go for fungal enthousiasts. A province in the south of China that bears loads of rain troughout the year. The rural villages within this province were mysteriously struck with a series of unexplainable deaths. Within three decades, an estimated 400 people died from cardiac arrest. What was even more strange about these series of deaths was that in hours leading up to the death, most victims experienced the same symptoms.

Years later, in 2010, scientists pointed the finger at the "little white mushroom". It contained three toxic amino acids that were the likely cause. However, this didn't explain everything, since an estimated 35 kilograms of this mushroom would be needed to be fatal to humans. It would also be the first fungus to cause heart complications.
