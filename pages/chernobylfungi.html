Fungi in Chernobyl

← Back

We all know about the Chernobyl disaster. The nuclear accident that happened in 1986 in a nuclear power plant in Ukraine. The radioactivity in the area is one of the highest in the world. After the disaster many plants and fungi have already reclaimed the destroyed land.

However, there are a group of fungi that have been found within the cooling waters of the nuclear power plant. Cryptococcus neoformans is primarily known for causing serious infections in immunocompromised humans. It not only survives in the radioactive environment, it seeks out the radioactivity to feed upon it. Experiments show fungi like C. neoformans grow faster in an environment which has 500 times more radiation than our normal environment.

This behaviour suggest that at a certain point, there was a more radiation on earth or a place where radiation was more privilent, causing this fungus to evolve these properties.
