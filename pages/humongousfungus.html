Humongous fungus

← Back

In Oregon's Malheur National forest there lies a secret under the soil. A specimien of Armillaria ostoyae that is so large it might be the largest organism on the planet. It covers 9 square kilometers of the soil with its mycelium network. Wieghing over 30000 metric tonnes, it may also be the heaviest.

It must have taken years for this parasite to spread out this far, which could in turn make it over 3000 years old. Meaning this single organism has seen generations upon generations of human civilization, possibly harvesting its fruiting bodies. A. ostoyae is an edible species and has no doubt fed some humans during its lifespan.
