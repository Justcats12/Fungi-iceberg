Atkinson's lost Inocibe

← Back

George F. Atkinson was an American botanist and mycologist who discovered many new species. One of these was Inocybe olpidiocystis, a lawn mushroom which he found in 1902. After Atkinson's death in 1918, the species was published formally by a friend.

Since that day in 1902 nobody has ever found a fruiting body of I. olpidiocystis again. That's why it's nicknamed "Atkinson's lost Inocibe".

There was hope in 1985, when a field guide contained a picture of the Inocibe, but there is no evidence to confirm this is truly I. olpidiocystis.
