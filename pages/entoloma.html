Entoloma

← Back

The genus Entoloma is strange in the sense that it's so diverse. While in other families of the basidiomycetes likely took years to evolve into gilled mushrooms and puffballs, this genus has both. It also has saprobic, mycorhizzal and parasitic species. More specifically, species that parasitize fruiting bodies of other fungi. This behaviour often takes millions of years to evolve, yet for Entoloma it's present in one singular genus.

It's diversity has made it practically impossible to classify the different species of the Entolomataceae family. While research is pending on this strange genus, it remains a mystery how such closely related species can be so versitile.

Some Entoloma species are edible, while others are poisonous. The general advice is to just not try picking any.
