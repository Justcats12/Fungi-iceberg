Prototaxites

← Back

Prototaxites aren't at all fungi, according to recent studies, but still belong on this list. Simply because they belong nowhere else. At first we thought they were trees, but they don't seem to belong to the plant kingdom. They are heterotrophes meaning they have to consume organic matter, just like us. So scientists thought they were fungi, which also didn't seem to be true. They lived at a point in time where no large land animals existed yet. They towered upto 8 meters tall above all the vegetation. Even though they existed 16 times longer than the genus homo, we don't know what kingdom they belong to.
