The dark magic mushroom

← Back

The fungal kingdom is known mainly for two kinds of toxins. First of all, the kind that destroys cells and kills you in the span of a week. Secondly, the kind that expands your mind. The most known amongst these are amatixins and psilocybin.

Due to horizontal gene transfer, which refers to the transfer of genetic material without traditional reproduction, a species has appeared which has both amatoxins and psilocybin. Galerina indica, discovered in 2014 has been shown to contain both of these compounds in a recent study by Kunik A. et al. The fungus has been dubbed "the dark magic mushroom" for this reason.
