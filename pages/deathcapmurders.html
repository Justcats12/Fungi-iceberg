2023 Death cap murders

← Back

On 29 July 2023, Erin Patterson had prepared beef wellington with mushroom sauce for her relatives. Of the four visitors only one would survive. Patterson had chosen a quite unique weapon for this murder, because she had a sinister plan to get away with it. She had put pieces of death cap in the sauce. She would have gotten away with it if weren't for Ian Wilkinson, the husband of her in-law's sister, who survived after a long struggle in the hospital. After getting arrested, she tried to play the excuse that she had bought them from an asian store and that there was a disasterous mixup. Other than the surviving key witness, the police had found a freeze-drying machine that Patterson had disposed of. Initially she lied about ever owning it, but later confessed that was a lie in court. The prosecution accused her of using this machine to turn the death caps into powder and putting it in the sauce.

She now serves three life sentences in prison.

The most interesting part about this story is that it's the only of it's kind in recent history. Perhaps the death cap is a rare method, or maybe those who use it tend to get away with it.
