Lenin was a mushroom

← Back

In may of 1991 Leningrad television televised a hoax that Vladimir Lenin was a mushroom.

The story told by Kuryokhin, who impersonates a historian, goes that Lenin consumed so many psychedelic mushrooms that he himself turned into a mushroom and a radio wave.

The hoax was a parody on theories about the death of Sergei Yesenin, which one television host claimed was murdered.
