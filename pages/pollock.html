The murder of Steven Hayden Pollock

← Back

Steven Hayden Pollock was a mycologist who focussed on magic mushrooms. Pollock was shot on 3 September 1977. Even though the police gathered substantial evidence, the district attorney Terry McDonald refused to prosecute for unknown reasons. In 2013, when the journalist Hamilton Morris tried to report that the detectives reached out to the wrong DA, the D.E.A. claimed that all records of the case were destroyed.

The police alledgedly burned his whole collection of magic mushrooms, possibly brining the species Pollock had discovered (Psilocybe tampanensis) to extinction. However, it had survived and is widely distributed in the Netherlands.

You can kill a man, but you can't kill his idea.
