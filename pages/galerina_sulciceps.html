Galerina sulciceps

← Back

Galerina sulciceps is an especially poisonous mushroom, dubbed to be the "most poisonous mushroom known to man". Concentrations of amatoxin and possibly other toxins being so high that death occurs within three days, as opposed to destroying angels which can take up to a week to be fatal. The symptoms are also not consisten with those of other amatoxin-carrying mushrooms, described as "pins and needles".

G. marginata, A similar species that grows in most places in the wolrd, shows symptoms more similar to those of the death cap. What these mushrooms do have in common is that, unlike the death cap, they can both be cultivated. For G. sulciceps this often happens accidentally within greenhouses. Because of this it has received the nickname "Greenhouse galerina". Outside of greenhouses it is found in tropical climates, mostly in Indonesia.
