Salem witch trials

← Back

Between 1692 and 1693 there were a series of witch trials Massachusetts. The trials have been dubbed the Salem Witch Trials. During these, 200 people had been accused of witchcraft of which 150 were jailed and 20 more executed.

While it's unclear what factors played what role in the mass hysteria of the witch trials, one contributing factors might have been ergot. This is a fungal infection mainly targetting rye, which can turn it toxic when consumed. The symptoms include hallucinations and painful spasms, which could explain some behaviours of people during the trials.
