Ötzi the Iceman

← Back

Ötzi was a prehistoric natural mummy of a man who lived over 5000 years ago. He was shot with an arrow and died, after which he was covered in ice and found again in 1991, well preserved.

Ötzi was carying Tinder mushrooms, which he likely used to start fires. He also carried Birch polypore, likely to be used as a medicine. This shows that the medicinal properties of fungi were understood way before the discovery of penicilin.
