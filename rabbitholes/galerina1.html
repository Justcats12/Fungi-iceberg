Galerina sp. 1

← Back

Galerina sp. 1, also known as FLAS-F-73457, is a psilocybin containing Galerina within the subgenus Mycenopsis. It was discovered in Mississippi for a study about psilocybin containing galerinas. It was found by Andrew Kunik and can be found on iNaturalist under observation 219241754. The pictures on that page are protected by copyright so I won't put them here (the image on this page is another unidentified galerina).
